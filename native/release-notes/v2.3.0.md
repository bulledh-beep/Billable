- **A coordinator for every big job.** Turn on Coordinator for a chat. It handles quick things itself and hands bigger work to jobs, each its own chat you can open and steer. It sends quick read-only lookups for research and picks each job's model and effort.
- **Plans you approve.** Before a bigger push, the coordinator shows the plan, and you can drop any job before it starts.
- **Decisions as cards.** When something is your call, the agent asks with 2 to 4 options and its recommendation. One tap answers, even later.
- **Review ready.** A job tells you when its work is ready to check. Approve it or ask for changes.
- **Project memory.** Your standing preferences are saved to the project, and every chat in it follows them.
